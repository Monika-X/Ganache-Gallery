Common Questions

Studio Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about cake transport, dietary options, and deposit policies.

How far in advance should I book my wedding cake?

We recommend booking 3 to 6 months in advance for peak wedding season (May through October). We limit our atelier production to 4 major wedding sculptures per weekend to guarantee undivided craftsmanship.

Do you accommodate gluten-free, vegan, or nut-free requirements?

Yes. We offer organic gluten-free almond flour sponges and vegan dark chocolate ganache formulations. While our kitchen handles tree nuts, we maintain strict sanitized equipment protocols for allergen safety.

How does delivery & on-site venue setup work?

All multi-tiered cakes are delivered in our custom climate-controlled vehicles by our atelier setup specialists. We assemble final sugar florals and gold leaf details directly at your venue display table.
