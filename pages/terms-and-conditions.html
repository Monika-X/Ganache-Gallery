Atelier Terms & Client Agreements

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

1. Retainer & Payment Schedule

To reserve an atelier production date on our master calendar, a non-refundable 50% retainer and a signed design agreement are required. The remaining 50% balance must be settled no later than 14 business days prior to your scheduled event date.

Orders requested fewer than 14 days in advance require full payment upon confirmation and are subject to expedited artisan sourcing fees.

2. Design Brief Finalization & Revisions

All custom cake dimensions, tier counts, flavor choices, and structural color schemes must be finalized in writing at least 21 days prior to delivery. Modifications requested after this deadline will be accommodated at Chef Hélène’s discretion and may incur additional sculpting fees.

3. Cancellations & Rescheduling Policy

If an event must be cancelled or postponed, please notify us immediately in writing. Retainers are non-refundable but may be transferred to a future date within 12 months, subject to atelier calendar availability and 30 days advance notice.

Cancellations made within 14 days of the event date are non-refundable as specialty ingredients and custom sugar florals will have already been handcrafted.

4. Refrigerated Delivery & On-Site Setup

Ganache Gallery assumes full responsibility for the structural integrity of your cake during transit and on-site assembly by our team. Once the cake has been set up at your venue and signed off by the venue coordinator, responsibility transfers to the venue management.

Cakes must be displayed in a climate-controlled room under 22°C (71°F) away from direct sunlight, heating vents, or outdoor elements.

5. Dietary Disclaimers & Allergen Safety

While we offer gluten-conscious and nut-conscious recipes, our atelier uses equipment that processes wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, peanuts, and tree nuts. Clients are responsible for notifying their event guests of potential allergen exposure.
