Bespoke Wedding Sculptures

Your wedding cake should be a magnificent reflection of your personal love story. We work directly with your wedding planner, florist, and venue team to ensure perfect color matching and architectural stability.

Features include custom tier sizing, hand-painted bas-relief patterns, translucent sugar petals, and climate-controlled venue delivery.

Luxury Grazing & Dessert Tables

Transform your event reception with an opulent dessert table featuring a grand centerpiece macaron pyramid, artisanal dessert jars, mini fruit tarts, and hand-rolled Valrhona truffles.

Our styling team provides custom pedestals, crystal glassware, floral styling, and menu callout cards matching your aesthetic theme.

Book Dessert Table Styling

Corporate & Brand Gala Gifting

Elevate your brand activations, fashion show favors, and high-net-worth client appreciation gifts with custom-embossed macaron coffrets and signature chocolate boxes.
