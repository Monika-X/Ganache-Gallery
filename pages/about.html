Our Origins

Born in a Parisian Kitchen, Perfected for Global Galas

Founded in 2012 by Master Pastry Chef Hélène Mercier after years of apprenticeship at renowned Parisian pâtisseries, Ganache Gallery was built on a single uncompromising pillar: that fine dessert is an edible art form requiring absolute mastery over both flavor and structure.

What began as an intimate boutique studio in Paris has grown into a world-class luxury atelier trusted by royal families, international event directors, and couples seeking unforgettable wedding cake centerpieces.

Uncompromising Standards

Our Culinary Ingredients & Philosophy

We never shortcut our baking process. We source the world's most prized raw ingredients for pure flavor integrity.

Rare Origin Cocoa & Vanilla

Exclusive Valrhona grand cru dark chocolate from Madagascar & Ecuador, paired with hand-scraped Tahitian vanilla pods.

Organic Wild Orchards

Wild raspberries harvested from Provence, Amalfi Meyer lemons, and organic Sicilian Bronte pistachios.

Zero Artificial Additives

All tints are derived from natural fruit powders, matcha, and beetroot extractions. No artificial colorings ever.

Our Journey

Atelier Milestones & Evolution

Fourteen years of continuous innovation in sugar sculpting and pastry science.

2012

Establishment in Paris

Chef Hélène opens her first 400 sq. ft. atelier studio on Rue Saint-Honoré focusing on custom macarons.

2017

Best Wedding Cake Atelier Award

Awarded Gold Medal by the International Pastry Association for revolutionary sugar paper sculpting.

2022

Launch of Private Tasting Suites

Opened our VIP Champagne Tasting Parlors and courier tasting box program for worldwide clients.

2026

Sustainable Zero-Waste Bakery Certification

Achieved 100% solar-powered kitchen operation and zero organic landfill waste certification.
