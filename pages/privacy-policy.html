Privacy Policy & Client Data Protection

Effective Date: August 26, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a custom design brief, book a private tasting parlor session, or subscribe to our pasty journal digest, Ganache Gallery collects essential contact details including your full name, email address, phone number, event venue location, dietary preferences, and event date.

We do not store full credit card numbers on our local servers. All financial transactions are processed using PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways.

2. Cookies & LocalStorage Usage

Our website utilizes lightweight client-side LocalStorage to save your visual preferences (Light/Dark mode theme selection and LTR/RTL layout orientation) and maintain your transient consultation inquiry brief bag across sessions.

You can clear your local preferences at any time by clearing your browser cache or toggling the theme controls in the header navigation.

3. VIP & High-Profile Event Anonymity

Ganache Gallery routinely serves royal families, celebrity galas, and high-net-worth private receptions. We strictly honor non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and offer total event confidentiality. No event photography will be shared on our digital channels without explicit written consent from the client or event director.

4. Technical Security Standards

All data transmitted through our website forms is encrypted using 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS) encryption. Access to client design briefs is strictly restricted to authorized atelier managers and Chef Hélène Mercier.

5. Your Rights & Privacy Officer Contact

Under GDPR and international privacy regulations, you have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or request immediate deletion of your consultation history from our active records.
