Our Atelier Story

Where Culinary Science Meets High-Fashion Design

Founded by Master Pastry Chef Hélène Mercier, Ganache Gallery was established to redefine luxury dessert craftsmanship. We believe every cake should be a gallery-worthy centerpiece that tastes even more extraordinary than it looks.

Using rare origin chocolates, wild berries, and organic edible flora, our team spends up to 40 hours sculpting single custom creations for discerning clientele worldwide.
